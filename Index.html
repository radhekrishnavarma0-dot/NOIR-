Our Story

AN ART FORM,
BOTTLED.

NOIRÉ was imagined as a celebration of individuality. Every fragrance tells a different story, combining carefully considered scent profiles with timeless visual design. From the first impression to the final lingering note, every detail is part of the experience.

NOIRÉ is a concept brand created by Houseofpri as a study in how dark photography, restrained gold, and quiet typography can make a fictional product feel real.
